🚨 Critical Product Insight

The Gap: 68% willing to pay premium for regenerative farming, but only 23% actually understand what it means for the product quality. Your biggest opportunity is connecting farming method to product benefits.

🔍 Consumer Concerns

Top Worry: "Does regenerative farming make fries taste different?"

Second Worry: "Is this just marketing or real change?"

Third Worry: "Why should I pay more for the same potato?"

💡 Product Opportunities

Education Gap: Explain how soil health = better potatoes

Taste Assurance: "Same great taste, better farming"

Value Messaging: Position as premium quality, not just sustainability

Bottom Line: Your regenerative farming initiative is working, but consumers need to understand WHY it makes better fries, not just better farms.
